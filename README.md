# Space Dodgers
 Space Dodgers is a space-themed arcade game built with Godot 4. Dodge incoming enemies, shoot your way through increasingly difficult waves, and survive until you reach the final boss. 

Defat the boss to earn your victory medal and unlock Never Ending Mode (Endless Mode). How long can you survive? 

# Features 
- **Progressive difficulty**: Face increasingly challenging enemy waves as your score increases.
- **Multiple enemy levels**: Progress through nine enemy levels.
- **Boss battle**: Fight a powerful final boss at 9,000 points.
- **Victory System**: Defeat the boss to earn a medal next to your best score and continue on endless mode.
- **Sound effects and music**: Enjoy background music, shooting sounds, and explosion effects. There are even sound effects for dying and killing the boss in the game!
- **Score tracking**: Earn points by destroying enemies.
- **Victory system**: Defeat the boss to earn a medal and unlock the endless mode.
- **Never ending mode**: Keep playing after defeating the boss.
- **Sound effects and music**: Enjoy background music, shooting sounds, and explosion effects. There are also sounds for dying and killing the boss in the game!
- **Score tracking**: Earn points by destroying enemies.
- **Game over and restart**: Try again when your run ends, your highest score stays with you in the game.

# How to PLay
Your objective is to survive the enemy waves, destroy opponents, increase your score, and defeat the final boss. 
1. Move your ship to avoid incoming enemies and projectiles (such as the bullets).
2. Shoot enemies to earn points (each enemy gives you 100 points).
3. Survive increasingly difficult waves.
4. Reach 9,000 points to encounter the boss.
5. Defeat the boss to earn your victory medal.
6. Continue playing in Endless Mode.

| Controls | Action |
|---|---|
|WASD | Move your ship | 
| SPACE | Shoot 
| SPACE after the game over | restart | 

# Getting started
**Play the game**
Download the latest Window build from the [Release](release/) section or the game's itch.io [page](https://chin-jie-hao.itch.io/space-dodging-game)

1. Download the Windows ZIP archive.
2. Extract the ZIP file.
3. Open the extracted folder.
4. Run Space_Dodgers_Windows.exe (please note that this game currently only supports Windows!!!)

Keep all exported game files together when running the executable.
